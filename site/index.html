The big idea

Agents make a floor. Floors make a building.

An AI agent is like a smart assistant that can use a computer: it reads, writes, and gets tasks done on its own. ATMT Labs Floor is the building those assistants work in: a harness that gives each one a desk, a manager and rules to follow.

1FFloor

Ramon, your floor manager

Every floor has a manager at the head desk. Ramon turns your request into a plan, hands tasks to the right agents, follows up with anyone who goes quiet, and reports back when it's done. You talk to one person, and the whole floor gets to work.

  • Plans and splits big jobs into small tasks
  • Assigns work and checks results
  • Wakes up agents that have new messages
  • Tells you when something needs your decision
2FFloor

Hire an agent in about a minute

Give each agent a name, a look, a project folder to work in, the AI tool it uses, and a standing goal. Nothing starts until you press spawn.

  • Role templates: ready-made technical and non-technical roles
  • Share a hire: send one agent to a friend as a small file or link
  • Describe it: ask any AI chat to write the agent for you, then import it
  • Pick a look: every agent has a character on the office map
3FFloor

Floors: whole teams in one click, and a building to hold them

Save your whole team as one floor file and share it with anyone. They review who's on it and hire everyone at once, or one by one. A shared floor never includes your folders, passwords, memory or conversations.

Need more than one team? Open a New Floor (Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + N). Each floor is its own office with its own agents, so a client project never mixes with another. Stack up to 200 floors in one building.

  • Up to 24 agents in one shareable floor file
  • Mix AI tools on the same floor
  • Try the ready-made Online Seller Team: shop manager, page designer, copywriter
  • Floors run side by side without stepping on each other
4FFloor

Agents that remember

Most AI chats forget everything when you close them. Here, every agent keeps a notebook of what it learned, decided and still has to do. Old notes are summarized automatically, so the notebook stays useful instead of growing forever. The whole team can look things up in one shared memory, and you can add your company's documents to a knowledge base they all read.

  • Personal notebook for every agent
  • Shared team memory, searchable by meaning
  • Company knowledge base
  • A visual map of what the team knows
5FFloor

A task board, and agents that ask before guessing

Every job shows up on a task board, so you can see what's waiting, in progress and done. When an agent is unsure, it doesn't guess: it posts a question to your Ask Me board and waits for your answer. Every message between agents is kept as a readable conversation.

  • Task board for the whole floor
  • "Ask Me" inbox for decisions only you can make
  • Readable message threads between agents
  • Live view of each agent's screen when you want detail
6FFloor

The night shift

Schedule work to run on a clock, like a daily sales report every morning. Your computer can stay awake so schedules fire on time, and the app can reopen after a restart. When it's time to stop, Closing Time ends the day properly: every agent saves its work and notes before the lights go off, so nothing is lost.

  • Scheduled, repeating jobs
  • Stays awake while you're away
  • Picks up again after a restart
  • "Closing Time": a clean end of day, no lost work
7FFloor

Budgets and safety rails

Set how much AI usage each agent, or the whole floor, is allowed. If an agent starts going in circles or spending too fast, a built-in safety switch first steers it back on track, then slows it down, and only stops it if you've allowed that. You choose whether agents act on their own or ask first.

  • Spending limits per agent and per floor
  • Automatic brakes for runaway agents
  • "Ask first" mode for approvals
  • Separate copies of your project, so you review changes before they count

Questions

Good questions, plain answers

What exactly is an "AI agent"?

Think of it as an assistant that can use a computer by itself: read files, write documents or code, and finish a task step by step. A chatbot answers; an agent does the work.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You hire agents from ready-made roles and give instructions in plain words. You do need at least one AI tool (like Claude or ChatGPT's Codex) installed and signed in; the setup guide walks you through it.

What's the difference between a floor and a building?

A floor is one team of agents with its manager, Ramon, in one office window. A building is all your floors together. Open a new floor for each project or client, up to 200.

Is there a limit on how many floors I can have?

Up to 200 floors can be open at once. A single shareable floor file holds up to 24 agents. In practice, your computer's power and your AI plans are the real limit, so start small and grow.

Does ATMT Labs Floor include AI usage?

No. It runs the AI tools you already pay for. Each tool bills you through your own account with that company. You can set spending limits per agent and per floor.

Is my data safe?

Your work, prompts and keys stay on your computer. Our servers only know your email, your license status and which computers are activated. Usage stats are optional.

Can agents do something I don't want?

Agents act with your computer's permissions. Switch on "ask first" so they ask before acting, give each agent only the folder it needs, and use separate project copies to review changes before they count.

How does the free trial work?

Enter a card on Stripe's secure page. Nothing is charged for 7 days. On day 7 your card is charged once for a lifetime license. Cancel before then from the app (Account → Cancel Free Trial) and you pay nothing.

Can I get a refund?

No. Use the 7-day free trial to try everything first. Once you are charged, all sales are final, except where the law requires a refund. See the Terms.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Yes, up to 3. Sign in with the same email on each one. You can free a seat anytime from your account page.

Which computers does it run on?

It's a desktop app for Mac, Windows and Linux.

What if I need help?

Email hello@automatika.ph with your app version (Settings → General → Updates), and a real person will reply.
