How it works

Agents make a floor. Floors make a building.

  1. 01

    Hire agents

    Pick a ready-made role or describe one. Each agent gets its own desk, folder, notebook and inbox.

  2. 02

    Give Ramon a goal

    Your manager splits it into tasks, hands them out, follows up and tells you when something needs you.

  3. 03

    Stack floors

    One floor per project or client, up to 200. Agents on different floors can work together.

Questions

Plain answers

What is an "AI agent"?

An assistant that can use a computer by itself: read files, write documents or code, and finish a task step by step. A chatbot answers; an agent does the work.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You hire agents from ready-made roles and give instructions in plain words. You need at least one AI tool (like Claude or ChatGPT's Codex) installed and signed in; the setup guide walks you through it.

Floor or building, what's the difference?

A floor is one team of agents in one office window. A building is all your floors together, up to 200. Ramon manages the whole building, and agents can message agents on other floors.

Does DevLabs include AI usage?

No. It runs the AI tools you already pay for, billed through your own accounts. You can set spending limits per agent and per floor.

Is my data safe?

Your work, prompts and keys stay on your computer, and there is no account. Agents act with your computer's permissions, so use "ask first" mode, give each agent only the folder it needs, and review changes on separate project copies.

Which computers, and where do I get help?

Mac, Windows and Linux, on as many computers as you like. For help, email hello@automatika.ph with your app version (Settings → General → Updates), or open an issue.
