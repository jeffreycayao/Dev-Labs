1. The short version

  • Your work stays on your computer. Your projects, files, prompts, agent conversations and output, agent memory, and the keys you paste for connectors are never sent to us.
  • We keep only what licensing needs: your email, your name, your license status, and which computers are activated.
  • Card details go to Stripe. We never see your full card number.
  • We don't sell or share personal data, and we don't use it for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Usage statistics are anonymous and optional. You can turn them off in the App.

3. Data from Google sign-in

You can sign in with your Google account. We request only these standard scopes: openid, email and profile. We don't request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Contacts or any other Google service.

  • Only for sign-in and licensing. We use Google user data only to sign you in, run your account and license, and provide support.
  • Never sold, never used for advertising. We don't sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to data brokers or information resellers.
  • Never used to train AI. We don't use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalised or non-personalised AI or machine-learning models.

  • Protected with encryption in transit (HTTPS) and access limited to the people who run the Service.

Remove our access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. Removing access stops new sign-ins with Google. To delete the data we already hold, see section 12.

4. Data from Yahoo sign-in

If you sign in with Yahoo, we request the same standard scopes (openid, email, profile) and receive the same three things: your Yahoo account ID, verified email address and name. We use them exactly as described for Google above. You can remove our access in your Yahoo account's security settings.

5. Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. You enter your card on Stripe's page, not ours. Stripe sends us only what we list in section 2. Stripe processes your payment data under its own privacy policy, including for fraud prevention.

6. What stays on your computer

The App runs on your computer. Your projects, files, prompts, agent conversations and output, agent memory, floor and hire files, and connector keys stay there. Connector keys are encrypted with your operating system's keychain.

Your AI tools are separate services. The agents are AI tools you install and sign in to yourself (such as Claude Code, Codex, Gemini or Cursor). When an agent works, that tool sends your prompts and files to its own company, under that company's terms and privacy policy, not ours. The same applies to connectors such as Pancake POS and Webcake. Read their policies to know how they handle your data.

7. Anonymous usage statistics

If you allow it, the App sends anonymous usage events (for example, "an agent was started") to PostHog Cloud (United States). Events carry a random install ID created on your computer, and no email, name, account ID, IP address, location, file names, prompts or output. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → General → Anonymous usage stats, or by setting the DO_NOT_TRACK environment variable. Builds not published by us send nothing.

10. How long we keep it

  • Account and license data: while your account exists.
  • Payment and purchase records: as long as tax and accounting law requires, even after you delete your account.
  • Activated computers: until you remove the computer or delete your account.
  • Sign-in sessions: up to 30 days; refresh tokens up to 90 days.
  • Request logs: for the period our hosting provider keeps them.

11. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what data we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • export it in a common format;
  • stop or limit how we use it, or object to a use based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw consent (for anonymous statistics, just switch them off).

Email our Data Protection Officer at support@automatika.ph. We answer within 30 days and may ask you to confirm the request from your account email.

Philippines: you have the rights of a data subject under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), and can complain to the National Privacy Commission.

EU, EEA and UK: you have the rights above under the GDPR / UK GDPR, and can complain to your local data protection authority.

California and other US states: we don't sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we don't use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you. We won't treat you differently for using your rights.

12. Deleting your account

Email hello@automatika.ph from your account email and ask us to delete your account. We delete your account data, including data received from Google or Yahoo, within 30 days, except records the law requires us to keep (such as payment records).

Deleting your account deletes your license. Computers already activated with a lifetime license keep working offline (see the Terms), but can't be activated again.

You can remove computers yourself, and cancel a trial, at auth.labs.atmt.dev/account. To remove data from your own computer, uninstall the App and delete its folders.

13. Security

  • All traffic uses HTTPS.
  • Tokens and unlock codes are stored hashed.
  • License files are signed and bound to one computer.
  • Connector keys are encrypted with your operating system's keychain and never leave your computer.
  • Access to production data is limited to the people who run the Service.

If we learn of a breach that affects your data, we'll notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

14. Children

The Service is only for people aged 21 or older. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 21. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we'll delete it.

15. Changes

We'll post changes on this page and update the date at the top. For material changes, we'll tell you on this website or in the App before they take effect.
