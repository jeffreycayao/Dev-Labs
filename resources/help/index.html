About Jepoy Floor AI
AI agents · your computer · your team

An office of AI helpers, working while you sleep.

Jepoy Floor AI turns the AI assistant you already use into a small team. Each helper — we call them agents — sits at a desk on a pixel-art office floor, works on its own task, and reports back. You stay the boss: you give the work, you review the results, you decide what goes live.

Runs on your computerYou approve what mattersPhilippine editionBuilt & supported in PH

What is Jepoy Floor AI?

Think of it as a virtual office for AI assistants. Normally you chat with one AI at a time and wait. In Jepoy Floor AI you can have several — a programmer, a marketer, a researcher — each doing their own job at the same time, and one manager (Ramon) who keeps everyone coordinated.

🏢The floorYour workspace. Every agent you add appears at a desk, and you can watch them walk, work and take coffee breaks.
🧑‍💼Ramon, the bossAlways at the head desk. Tell Ramon what you want; he breaks it into tasks and hands them to the right agent.
🤖AgentsAI helpers with a name, a role and a goal. Each one runs its own AI session, so they work in parallel.
🔒Local-firstThe app, your files and your agents' memory stay on your computer. Only the AI requests go to the AI company you chose.

A tour of the screen

Top bar

Shows the version (right now v—) and whether auto mode is on. The buttons on the right open settings, tools and full-screen view.

The office floor (centre)

Every agent is a character at a desk. A speech bubble above their head shows what they're doing right now ("reading files", "running tests"). When they take a break they chat in the pantry — just for fun.

Some objects are shortcuts: the calendar on Ramon's wall opens Triggers, the wall boards open Tasks, and the wall clock is "clock out" (close the app).

Agent cards (bottom)

One card per agent with its status: idle, working, waiting or blocked. Click a card to select that agent. + add agent hires a new one.

Command Center (right side)

When you select Ramon or an agent, the right panel shows its live work. Tabs include:

  • Terminal — the agent's live session (what it's typing and doing).
  • Tasks and Ask me — the to-do board and questions waiting for you.
  • Triggers, Memory, Activity, Skills, Workers — schedules, what the team remembers, history, extra abilities, and helper agents.
Queue & message box (bottom right)

Type instructions here and press send. Attach files with + files or speak with voice. If the agent is busy, the message joins the queue and runs next.

Your first 10 minutes

  1. Open or create a workspace. The first screen asks for a harness config — the folder that holds this office. Pick the suggested one or create a new one.
  2. Answer "Who are you?" Choose technical or non-technical. This only changes how the app explains things and which role templates it suggests first.
  3. Connect an AI engine. Follow the prompts to install and sign in to your AI service. If something is missing, the app shows an Install card and does it for you.
  4. Meet Ramon. He's already at the head desk. Say hello in the message box: "Hi Ramon, what can you help me with?"
  5. Add your first agent. Click + add agent, pick a role template, choose the project folder, and press spawn.
  6. Give a small, clear task. For example: "Read my product list and write 5 Facebook captions in Taglish."
  7. Review the result before using it. Agents are fast, not perfect.

Adding agents

Click + add agent. The form has four short steps:

StepWhat you fill in
1 · IdentityName, character and colour — so you can tell agents apart on the floor.
2 · WorkspaceThe project folder the agent may work in. Optionally, isolation gives it a private copy so it can't disturb others' work.
3 · EngineWhich AI service and model it uses. Leave the default if unsure.
4 · BriefingA job description and a standing goal — the agent's "job post".

The fast way: role templates

Under Role templates, pick a job — Jr. Programmer, Security Auditor, Scrum Master, Marketing Executive, Social Media Manager, Bookkeeper and more. Every field is filled in for you, including safe limits (for example, the Bookkeeper never moves money, the Social Media Manager never posts directly). Review, then press spawn.

You can also import hire (load a ready-made agent file) or use generate one with AI to design a custom role.

Giving work & checking results

Write good instructions
  • Be specific: say what you want, for whom, and what "done" looks like.
  • One task at a time per agent. Big jobs? Give them to Ramon to split up.
  • Give examples of the style you like.

✅ "Write 3 short product descriptions for our ube cheese pandesal, friendly Taglish, under 60 words each."
❌ "Do marketing."

Watch progress

Speech bubbles and the card status tell you what's happening. Select an agent to see its live terminal. Anything waiting on you appears under Ask me.

Review before you use

Always read the output before sending it to customers, publishing it, or deploying code. You can reply to the agent to request changes — it keeps the context.

Stop or pause an agent

Select the agent and use stop after this step, or close its card. Closing the app (or clicking the wall clock) asks before stopping agents that are still working.

Working with Ramon

Ramon is your manager. Instead of instructing every agent yourself, tell Ramon the outcome you want:

"Ramon, we're launching a new ube flavour next week. Have the team prepare the product page copy, 5 social posts and a customer email. Show me drafts by Friday."

He plans the work, assigns it, follows up, and brings back what needs your decision. You can also talk to him by voice using the talk button on his card.

AI engines & costs

Each agent uses an AI engine. Supported engines include Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, Gemini, Kimi, Qwen, GitHub Copilot, Cursor and OpenCode — which can use many models, including GLM (Z.ai Coding Plan), DeepSeek and local models via Ollama.

💸 Costs: AI services charge you directly — by subscription or per use. Every active agent uses your plan. Watch the usage counter, start with few agents, and set token limits for agents that run for a long time.

Safety & good habits

📁Only share the right folderPoint agents at a project folder — never your whole drive, Desktop or Downloads.
🔑Use limited API keysCreate a separate key for Jepoy Floor AI with a spending limit, so a mistake can't run up a big bill.
🛑Auto mode with careAuto mode lets agents act without asking. Use it only on projects you trust, and keep a backup.
👀Review before publishingAgents draft; people approve. Don't let agents post, email customers or pay anything on their own.
🧾No secrets in chatDon't paste passwords, bank details or customer personal data into messages.
💾Keep backupsUse Git or regular copies of your project folder so any change can be undone.

Troubleshooting

An agent is stuck on "working" for a long time

Select it and look at the terminal. It may be waiting for an answer or a permission. Check Ask me. If it's truly stuck, use stop after this step and send the task again.

"Engine not installed" or sign-in problems

Use the Install card in the agent's terminal, then sign in when asked. Settings → Prerequisites lists everything that's missing.

I don't see my AI models (e.g. GLM) in the list

For OpenCode, sign in to the provider inside OpenCode first. Jepoy Floor AI reads the models you have access to automatically — wait a few seconds after opening the model list. You can also type any model name in the command field.

The app opened a "Select a harness config" screen

That's the workspace picker. Choose the one marked current to continue where you left off.

Windows says "unknown publisher" or macOS blocks the app

Test builds may not be signed yet. On Windows click More info → Run anyway; on Mac right-click the app → Open. Only do this for installers you received from Jepoy Floor AI directly.

Top 20 FAQ

01 How is Jepoy Floor AI licensed?

Jepoy Floor AI is free and open source under the MIT License. It is a fork of Munder Difflin by Chaitanya Giri. There is no account or sign-in. Your AI service (Claude, ChatGPT, Z.ai, etc.) is billed separately by that provider under its own pricing. Source code: github.com/jeffreycayao/Dev-Labs.

02 Do I need to know how to code?

No. Choose non-technical at setup and use the non-technical role templates — marketing, content, support, research, admin, HR and bookkeeping.

03 What can agents actually do?

Read and write files in your project folder, research, draft content, write and test code, organise information and report back. They work through the AI engine you chose, inside the folder you allowed.

04 How many agents should I run?

Start with Ramon plus 1–2 agents. Add more once you see how much work (and cost) each one creates.

05 Does it keep working when I close the app?

No — agents run while the app is open and your computer is awake. Leave it open (and plugged in) for long jobs. Closing the app asks first if agents are still working.

06 Can agents make mistakes?

Yes. AI can be confidently wrong. Always review output before you publish, send, pay or deploy anything.

07 Can I use it in Taglish or Filipino?

Yes. Write your instructions in Taglish, Filipino or English, and ask for replies in the language you want.

08 Can I use GLM, DeepSeek or local (offline) models?

Yes, through OpenCode (and Crush or pi). Sign in to the provider in OpenCode — for example the Z.ai Coding Plan for GLM — and pick the model in step 3 of Add Agent. Local models run through Ollama.

09 Where is my data stored?

On your computer: the app's settings folder plus the workspace (harness config) folder you chose. Agents' work stays in your project folders.

10 🔒 Is my data sent anywhere?

Only what an agent needs is sent to the AI service you chose — for example, the text of a file it's working on. Jepoy Floor AI has no server of its own collecting your files or chats.

11 🔒 How are my API keys protected?

Keys you enter under Settings → AI Engines and integration credentials are encrypted with your operating system's keychain (Windows DPAPI / macOS Keychain). The app's screens can't read them back, and a key is only given to an agent when it starts. Note: the voice-dictation (Free Flow) key is currently kept in the app's settings file; an update will encrypt it too — use a limited key for it.

12 🔒 Can an agent see my API key?

An agent receives the key it needs to call its AI service, so a misbehaving agent could read it. That's why we recommend a separate, spending-limited key for Jepoy Floor AI, and turning auto mode on only for trusted projects.

13 🔒 Can agents access my whole computer?

They work in the project folder you give them. In auto mode they can run commands without asking, so keep them on project folders — never your whole drive — and keep backups.

14 🔒 What is auto mode and is it safe?

Auto mode skips "may I?" prompts so agents work faster. It's safe on projects you trust and back up. Avoid it when agents read content from untrusted sources (random websites, unknown files), which could contain hidden instructions.

15 🔒 Does Jepoy Floor AI track me?

Jepoy Floor AI builds ship without usage tracking. The app contains no hidden reporting to the original project or to third parties.

16 🔒 Is customer or personal data safe to use?

Follow the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173): share only what's necessary, remove personal details where you can, and check your AI provider's data policy before sending customer information.

17 🔒 What if I think a key has leaked?

Revoke it immediately on the AI provider's website, create a new one, enter it again in Settings → AI Engines, and check the provider's usage page for anything unusual.

18 How do I update the app?

When updates are published the app tells you in the top bar. Otherwise install the newest version you receive from Jepoy Floor AI over the old one — your settings and agents are kept.

19 Can I have separate offices for different projects?

Yes. Use File → New Floor or create a new harness config at startup. Each has its own agents, memory and history.

20 Who do I contact for help?

Open an issue at github.com/jeffreycayao/Dev-Labs or visit jepoy.dev. Include your version number (top of this page) and a screenshot.
